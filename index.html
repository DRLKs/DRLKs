About

I'm a DevOps engineer working remotely from Spain, currently an Associate DevOps Engineer at Certinia. My work sits between development and operations: designing and monitoring secure AWS environments with Terraform, automating CI/CD with Jenkins, and building the automation that keeps a data-driven team shipping reliably.

I came to operations from the data side. At BHS Corrugated I built PostgreSQL-backed data workflows and pushed predictive models toward production through CI/CD and MLOps practices. In hackathons I usually take the infrastructure role: containers, reverse proxies, and cloud deployments on AWS and OCI. I'm at home in Linux and over SSH.

Rust is my language of choice for tooling and systems work, from a zero-dependency optimization framework to a computer-vision poker assistant. I hold a B.Eng. in Software Engineering from the University of Malaga, I'm studying an M.Sc. in Language Technologies at UNED, and I completed Coursera's Architecting Solutions on AWS.
